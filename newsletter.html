Campus Chronicles: Urja 2026

The 10th edition of Thapar's beloved sports fest roared to life across three electrifying days, and we were right there in the thick of it, with notebooks in hand, cameras rolling, occasionally getting hit by stray volleyballs. Do you like your sports served with a side of gully nostalgia? Does the memory of chalked boundaries, improvised rules, and evening cricket under a fading sun still make you smile? With heart, humour, and a little chaos (the team is well and happy, we promise). This one's for every player who pushed through exhaustion, every supporter who screamed their lungs out, and every soul who showed up. Welcome to Urja 2026. Enjoy the ride."
