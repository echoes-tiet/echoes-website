<p>Amphora, the Eighth edition of our Magazine, celebrates the vibrant tapestry of India on our campus. Languages dance in the air, festivals paint our dorms with joy, and regional music fills the hallways. We explore how these diverse threads intertwine. Food, a cornerstone of Indian life, becomes a shared experience. Hostel rooms transform into a ‘mini India,’ each dish a story of home. Discover how all these seemingly dissimilar traditions – clothing, music, art – create a symphony of inclusivity. Amphora dives into this rich mix, showcasing how our university life becomes a kaleidoscope of experiences, enriching us all.</p>
