<p>Echoes took a step ahead and tried to encapture the mind-boggling intricacies of the past, present, and future. The depiction of what we have left behind and what lies ahead makes up for the theme of this edition. "Timed Voyage" is a treat for anyone enraptured by the concept of time. No doubt time has shaped through the years by and for the society. Game through decades and the timeline of TIET, followed by the brilliant creative pieces revolving around ions and ions of moments, build the heart of this edition. A walkthrough of how these transcending times have come to exist, where we stand, and where we go from here all comes together under one single title.</p>
