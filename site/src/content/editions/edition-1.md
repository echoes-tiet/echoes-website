<p>The journey of the official student magazine for TIET began with catering to the freshers of the year 2020. 'Bienvenidos Freshers' aimed at welcoming the new members of TIET in a warm and quirky way. From the periodic table of societies to the map that covered the very steps of this campus, this magazine gave a glimpse of what it's like being a Thaparian. As you flip through the pages of this edition, you come across all the flagship events occurring throughout the year, put together in one place. There's also a place for people interested in the beauty of words. Shabdkosh showcased the various terms that form the core of what this campus becomes when it's full of budding students.</p>
