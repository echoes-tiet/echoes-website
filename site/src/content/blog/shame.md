<p><i>
It truly is a shame,<br>
How girls to the society are still a stigma and a bane.<br>
What I want is not so hard<br>
To be understood by a person with or without a legal card.<br>
<br><br>
It is a shame,<br>
For a few, girls are the ones who still hold the blame.<br>
It gives me goosebumps, for solely a topic as sensitive<br>
As this, has yet again failed to change the mindset that still remains primitive.<br>
<br><br>
It is a shame,<br>
That actions for these horrendous repetitive things are still the same.<br>
The culprits are sent for way too many inconclusive trials<br>
The point being, the rights of women are still in denial.<br>
<br><br>
It is a shame,<br>
Because it is not the culprits but the victims who are defamed.<br>
The laws were never any fair<br>
There still is a long way for just punishments to be declared.<br>
<br><br>
It is a shame,<br>
That not all have the fuming rage and the blazing flame.<br>
It forever has been like this and may go on- the society remains suppressive<br>
Not even thinking how this topic for some is so depressive.<br>
<br><br>
It is a shame,<br>
That men claim to have the rights to play unethical games.<br>
It is now the time to get the inner spirit stand against and inflame,<br>
It is now that women, voice out to disclaim.<br>
<br><br>
It is a shame,<br>
Because it is now or never to exclaim.<br>
It is now the time to not be ashamed,<br>
And to prove to the world that women can NEVER be tamed.<br>
<br><br>
It will no longer be a shame<br>
When women can do freely what-so-ever they may aim.<br>
When women with due respect, will have in society a say and a name,<br>
Then the world will know what they will become and proclaim!<br>

</i>
