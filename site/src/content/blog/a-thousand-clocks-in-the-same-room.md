<p>You open the door, and the first thing you hear is chaos. Ticking. Whirring. Clicking. Time hits you in the face. Definitely not like a breeze, but a storm.</p>

<p>A thousand clocks, ticking in protest. Some fast, some slow. Some ticking backward. Some not ticking at all. It’s a room where time isn’t measured, it argues. A battlefield of seconds, each clock with a personality louder than the last.</p>

<p>One clock, shaped like a Rubik’s Cube, spins wildly; its hands are confused. It ticks like your first semester in college: waking up in the wrong class, submitting assignments to the wrong portal, figuring out life with caffeine and panic.<br>
You chuckle. It knows you.</p>

<p>Next to it, a glossy smartwatch screams with beeps and vibrations. It’s the gym freak. The one who wakes you at 5 a.m., tracks every step, logs every calorie, and reminds you that your screen time is a crime.<br>
It doesn’t tick, it judges.</p>

<p>In the corner, there's a tiny alarm clock wrapped in fairy lights. Soft pink. Cute, but passive-aggressive. It ticks in lullabies, reminding you of the friend who says, “Take rest,” and then sends four PDFs on “How to Be Productive”.<br>
You both love and fear it.</p>

<p>Hanging crooked on the ceiling is a clock that plays lo-fi music. It ticks only between 2 a.m. and 5 a.m. (your sacred hours). It knows the pages of your journal, the playlist you don’t share, and the tears you blame on “something in your eye.”</p>

<p>But not all clocks are kind.</p>

<p>A rusted wall-clock ticks violently, stuck between 9:59 and 10:00, the moment before your biggest mistake. Maybe a breakup, a betrayal, or a choice you wish you could press Ctrl+Z on. It won’t move forward. It wants you to suffer.<br>
You look away.</p>

<p>And then there’s that clock. A blank one. No hands, no ticks. Just a mirror where the dial should be. You stare, and it stares back, reflecting the version of you you’re becoming, or maybe… avoiding.</p>

<p>The room swells with noise. Time spirals. Seconds turn to questions. Minutes blur into memories. You’re drowning in time; not lost, but multiplied.</p>

<p>And then...<br>
Silence.</p>

<p>Every clock dies at once.<br>
Except one.</p>

<p>It begins to tick. Slowly. Softly. Almost... shyly.<br>
It doesn’t belong to yesterday.<br>
It doesn’t race toward tomorrow.<br>
It lives entirely now.</p>

<p>You watch it, heart still. Maybe, just maybe, this is the version of you that’s been waiting all along; the one not defined by deadlines, mistakes, or even dreams.</p>

<p>The tick grows louder.<br>
Or was that your heartbeat?</p>

<p>And now, only one question remains:<br>
Will you follow the clock that ticks...<br>
or the one that finally lets you breathe?</p>

<p>Tick.<br>
Or is it... a tock?<br>
You decide.</p>
