---
title: "Doubt"
author: "Anamika Srivastava"
---

<p><i>
I wonder how the world <br>
gave you reasons enough to doubt,<br>
the smile that you are made up of <br>
and the constellations you’re all about.<br>
<br><br>
How can a mug of insecurities<br>
measure the depth of your soul,<br>
when you are not pieces shattered<br>
but a beautiful poetry as a whole?<br>
<br><br>
Though so often you fail <br>
to see the death of your eyes,<br>
or the love in your heart <br>
as you have seemed to master disguise.<br>
<br><br>
While there could always be someone<br>
out there better than you,<br>
or another who is rather less,<br>
perhaps a little grey and blue.<br>
<br><br>
And it’s this pain that you carry<br>
locked by your heart within,<br>
which rolls away down your eyes<br>
or is inked on your porcelain skin.<br>
<br><br>
But why should you walk<br>
along these comparisons all day,<br>
when you are a masterpiece in itself,<br>
flawed but beautiful in your own way?<br>
<br><br>
Because people are not made<br>
by cards of contrast,<br>
but by the choices they have made,<br>
for the existence in the past.<br>
<br><br>
I know it's been so long<br>
since I've seen you sway,<br>
to the rhythm of the songs<br>
that wash your pain away.<br>
<br><br>
Perhaps it might take years<br>
or maybe a lifetime too,<br>
to notice all the celestials <br>
that make up to you.<br>
<br><br>
And I can only ask,<br>
for you to love your own self,<br>
to open your chapters and read them aloud<br>
from the book your heart has shelved. <br>
<br><br>
So, I hope you learn to see<br>
the beauty in your eyes,<br>
in the curve of your smile<br>
and in the scars of your thighs.<br>
<br><br>
And maybe one day you'll love,<br>
every colour of your own rain,<br>
and have the courage to smile<br>
as you start all over again.<br>

</i>
</p>
