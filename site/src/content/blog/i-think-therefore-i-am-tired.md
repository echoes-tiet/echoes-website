<p>There are days I wake up feeling like someone else has already lived half of me.<br>
Like I’ve walked through corridors I don’t remember entering, nodded in rooms I don’t belong to, and spoken words that sound rehearsed even the first time I say them. It’s not that I dislike who I’ve become. It’s just , I don’t remember choosing her. Somewhere along the line, my life became a string of deliverables.<br>
Assignments, opportunities, forms, feedback. A well-oiled system of doing things that look good from the outside.<br>
And inside?<br>
Just a soft humming. Like a fridge running in a quiet kitchen. Always on. Never loud.<br>
But always there.</p>

<p>They call it burnout. But that’s too clean a word.<br>
What I feel is slower. Stranger.<br>
Like being halfway through a novel and realising you’ve been reading the same paragraph for weeks.</p>

<p>There was a version of me once , who did things simply because they moved her.<br>
She wrote for no audience. Spoke without rehearsing. Felt without bracing.<br>
I think she left quietly.<br>
And the worst part is:<br>
no one even noticed.</p>
