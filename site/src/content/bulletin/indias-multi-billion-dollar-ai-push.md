<p>
India is aggressively orchestrating a massive dual expansion across <strong>hardware
manufacturing</strong> and <strong>digital infrastructure</strong> to establish itself as a primary
global hub for artificial intelligence. Backed by government incentives and sovereign initiatives,
the country is coupling <em>local semiconductor fabrication</em> with an unprecedented surge in
<strong>high-density computing capacity</strong>.
</p>

<p>
<strong>$20 Billion Semiconductor Strategy:</strong> Under the <strong>Semicon India</strong>
initiative and the approved <strong>Semicon 2.0 framework</strong>, India has committed over
<strong>$20 billion</strong> across 12 approved semiconductor projects covering fabrication, assembly,
testing, and packaging (ATMP) units. This long-term industrial framework is designed to secure
<span>silicon sovereignty</span>, reduce reliance on fragile supply chains, and build native chip-making
resilience.
</p>

<p>
<strong>National AI Compute Layer:</strong> Supported by the <strong>IndiaAI Mission</strong>, the
government is democratizing access to enterprise-grade compute. The sovereign strategy focuses on
rolling out <em>subsidised access to tens of thousands of GPUs and TPUs</em> while developing
<span>sovereign foundational AI model clusters</span> to fuel domestic research, start-ups, and
strategic technology development.
</p>

<p>
<strong>Hyperscaler Expansion:</strong> Global tech giants are accelerating cloud infrastructure
commitments across key corridors. <strong>Amazon Web Services (AWS)</strong> has expanded its planned
infrastructure investment to over <strong>$21 billion by 2030</strong>, while <strong>Microsoft</strong>
has committed <strong>$17.5 billion</strong> toward cloud-AI scaling and technical skilling, supported
by deep regional network investments from <strong>Meta</strong>.
</p>

<p>
<strong>Domestic Conglomerate Bets &amp; Capacity Boom:</strong> Indian conglomerates like
<strong>Reliance Industries</strong> and <strong>Adani</strong> are deploying billions into
<em>hyperscale, green-powered AI data center campuses</em> to ensure native data residency.
Consequently, India’s installed data center capacity is projected to surge from
<strong>~1.2 GW toward multi-gigawatt scales by 2030</strong>, driven almost entirely by
<span>AI training and inference workloads</span>.
</p>

<p>
<strong>Summary:</strong> India’s multi-billion dollar push into chipmaking and AI infrastructure has
unlocked a major industrial shift. Driven by state-backed incentives and massive data center commitments
from Reliance, Adani, Amazon, and Microsoft, the country is building an end-to-end ecosystem—from silicon
fabrication to high-density compute—to secure technological sovereignty and power the global AI economy.
</p>
