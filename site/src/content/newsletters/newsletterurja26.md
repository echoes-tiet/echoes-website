<p>"Echoes brings you Campus Chronicles: Urja Edition 2026, and what a landmark edition it was. The 10th edition of Thapar's beloved sports fest roared to life across three electrifying days, and we were right there in the thick of it, with notebooks in hand, cameras rolling, occasionally getting hit by stray volleyballs. Do you like your sports served with a side of gully nostalgia? Does the memory of chalked boundaries, improvised rules, and evening cricket under a fading sun still make you smile? Then you're in exactly the right place. This edition draws its heart from that same raw, carefree spirit, the kind that needs no scoreboard to feel something. Wrapped in a bold, comic-inspired aesthetic, we've brought you stories from the track and the court, the carnival and the closing ceremony, the nail-biting football final and the cricket match that ended in "satisfied conversations around the boundary." From Pardeep Narwal setting the tone at the opening ceremony, to Thapar lifting the Overall Championship trophy, from the first-ever shooting event to an India-Pakistan thriller watched under the stars at the Open Air Theatre, we've captured it all. With heart, humour, and a little chaos (the team is well and happy, we promise). This one's for every player who pushed through exhaustion, every supporter who screamed their lungs out, and every soul who showed up. Welcome to Urja 2026. Enjoy the ride."</p>
