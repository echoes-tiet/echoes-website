<p>"The much anticipated 6th edition of the most inspiring sports event in North India, Urja, promised supreme athleticism and camaraderie. Back after years, TIET’s annual sports fest was a stage for college teams to showcase their talent by competing with the nation’s best. Echoes played a pivotal role in bringing Urja to life. We operated as a driving force, meticulously covering and documenting each facet of the event from the very minute to the unbelievable. This association forged a relationship between performance and emotions, preserving the precious moments of sportsmanship in an ever-eternal medium of words presented in our official Newsletter."</p>
